🚀 How to Use

Step 1: Sign Up or Log In

Create your secure account to access the malaria detection system. You can sign up as a hospital staff, lab technician, or medical professional. Your account ensures safe storage of patient records and analysis history.

Step 2: Upload an Image

Use our intuitive interface to upload a microscope image of red blood cells. Make sure the image is clear and of high resolution to allow accurate detection. Accepted formats include JPG, PNG.

Step 3: AI Diagnosis

Our advanced AI algorithm processes the image in seconds to detect signs of malaria. The system identifies parasitic infections in red blood cells and provides a confidence score for the diagnosis, minimizing human error.

Step 4: View and Download Report

Receive a detailed diagnostic report including infection stage, and recommended next steps. You can download the report as PDF or access it later from your dashboard. Useful for patient consultation and record-keeping.
